02

The Idea

Paper before pixels

A world is not designed. It is remembered — until it feels true.

Every Emberline world begins as a single sentence, handwritten on the worst paper we can find. If the sentence survives a week of arguments, it becomes a map. If the map survives a month, it becomes a world.

We believe constraints are kind. One accent colour. One weather. One wound in the history of a place. From that small seed, everything else grows — architecture, cuisine, grief, festivals, the way a character stands when it rains.

Nothing enters production until we can answer one question: what does this world want to say to the person holding the controller?

03

The Making

Craft, iteration, play

Four phases, repeated until it hurts, then once more. We prototype on paper, we playtest on Fridays, and we cut anything that does not serve the story.

PH.01

Sketch

Weeks of thumbnails, references, and bad drawings on purpose. We search for the silhouette of a world before its details.

PH.02

Prototype

Grey boxes and borrowed assets. A mechanic must be fun as a capsule with a lantern on it, or it will never be fun dressed up.

PH.03

Play

Every Friday, the whole studio plays the build. Every Friday, something we loved breaks. That is the point.

PH.04

Polish

The last ten percent takes forty percent of the time. Footsteps on black sand. The weight of a door. The pause before a line is spoken.
